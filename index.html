    Before you head out

    Planned dates, not guarantees. This is a November 2026 snapshot of the NC Wildlife schedule, last modified October 20 and retrieved October 23, 2026. Dates can change. Check the official schedule before traveling.

    Know the water. Pins do not identify parking, public access, or exact stocking spots. Respect private property and posted boundaries. Check current trout regulations and the official fishing map.

    November rules and licenses. During delayed harvest, no trout may be harvested or possessed; use only artificial lures with one single hook and do not possess natural bait while fishing. Check current regulations and posted signs. A Mountain Heritage Trout Waters license covers only specifically designated waters, not every water here.

    A different fall in 2026. NC Wildlife's October 22 announcement describes reduced trout availability after hatchery losses and the Setzer renovation, and warns that hurricane damage may have changed stocking locations and access. A date does not confirm a release or that a road, trail or park is open. Guides were researched October 23, 2026; fishing suggestions are recommendations, not catch predictions.

    The source of the North Carolina Wildlife Resources Commission Public Mountain Trout Waters data is North Carolina Wildlife Resources Commission. Boundaries are approximate and should not be used for regulatory purposes.
